# Mixamo

- Source: https://www.mixamo.com (Adobe account required)
- License: Mixamo / Adobe General Terms — royalty-free use of the animations in games and other projects, including commercial ones;
  the raw files must not be redistributed on their own (keep them in this private repository only).
- Download settings: Format "FBX for Unity (.fbx)", Skin "Without Skin", 30 fps, Keyframe Reduction "none";
  "In Place" on for walks, runs and climbs (the game moves the character itself).
- File names may stay as Mixamo gives them: the tag (Idle, Walk, Run, Attack, Hit, Stagger, Death, Climb) is found from the words
  in the name; a "<Tag>_" prefix overrides it.
